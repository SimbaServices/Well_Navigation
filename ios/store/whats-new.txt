What's New (App Store Connect → 1.0 → What's New, 4000-character limit):

Initial release of Well Navigation, workplace software for oil and gas teams.

Sign in with your work email to search and map wells, pipelines, and waste-disposal sites in Texas, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Louisiana. Pin wells, save a USGS map view for offline field use, and open Apple Maps or Google Maps for turn-by-turn.

This app does not sell subscriptions. Organization admins buy seats on the website in Safari.
