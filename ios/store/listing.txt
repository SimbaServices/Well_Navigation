Description (App Store Connect, 4000-character limit):
Well Navigation is workplace software for oil and gas teams. Sign in with your work email to search and map wells, pipelines, and waste-disposal sites in Texas, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Louisiana.

SEARCH
Look up wells by lease or well name, API number, or operator. Texas APIs start with 42-, New Mexico 30-, Oklahoma 35-, Louisiana 17-. Add an operator filter, then narrow by name or API. Sort the table and pin wells onto the map from a row, a selection, or the whole page.

Search pipelines by operator, P-5, or system name. Coverage includes Texas RRC T-4, New Mexico EIA and BLM right-of-way, Oklahoma EIA, and Louisiana EIA and BSEE. Lines are colored by commodity: gas, crude, HVL, product, and other.

Search commercial waste, UIC, and salt-water disposal sites by facility, operator, or permit (STF, R9, WP, and similar). Sources include Texas commercial waste, New Mexico SWD, Oklahoma UIC, and Louisiana injection.

MAP AND FIELD USE
Pin wells from search, including wellhead and toe coordinates when available. Switch base layers among Esri Imagery, Streets, Topo, OpenStreetMap, and USGS Topo. Toggle pipeline and waste-site overlays. Open Apple Maps or Google Maps for turn-by-turn.

Before you lose signal, tap Save this view to keep USGS tiles plus the last pipeline and waste overlay for that area on the device. Pinned wells stay available offline. Search and new lookups still need a connection. Esri layers stay online-only.

YOUR WORKSPACE
Save wells to your account, reopen recent searches, and manage your team. People on the same company email domain share one organization. Admins assign seats and roles. Delete your account anytime from Account → Delete account.

BILLING
This app is free to download. It does not sell subscriptions and does not collect payment. Organization admins buy seats on the website in Safari. The same signed-in workspace works on the web, iOS, and Android.

IMPORTANT
Records come from public regulatory and published sources and can be incomplete or out of date. Well and pipeline locations are not a substitute for a One Call / 811 locate, an as-built survey, or operator drawings. Call 811 before you dig.
