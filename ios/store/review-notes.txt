2. Purpose and target audience
================================================================================
Purpose: Well Navigation helps oil and gas professionals find and map wells, pipelines, and commercial waste / UIC / salt-water disposal sites so they can plan fieldwork and navigate to locations.

Problem it solves: Regulatory well, pipeline, and disposal records are scattered across state and federal public sources. Field and office staff need one signed-in search and map instead of jumping between portals and spreadsheets.

Value: Search by lease/well name, API number, or operator; pin wells on a map; open Apple Maps or Google Maps for turn-by-turn; before losing signal, tap Save this view to keep USGS tiles and pinned wells on the device.

Target audience: Oil and gas professionals (operators, land, field, HSE, consultants) who need TX / NM / OK / LA public well and infrastructure records. Catalog coverage is Texas, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Louisiana only.

4. External services, tools, and platforms (verified in code)
================================================================================
Hosting / app backend: https://wellnav.simba.services (first-party Well Navigation service loaded in WKWebView).

Authentication: first-party email/password sessions. Registration and password recovery send a 6-digit email OTP via the app’s own SMTP mail sender (mail.simba.services / WELLNAV_SMTP_FROM). Sign in with Apple and other third-party social logins are not offered because login is email/password only. The App Store iOS sign-up / sign-in flow does not send SMS; Twilio is not part of that flow.

Well and infrastructure public data compiled by the service (not a proprietary third-party well database):
- Texas Railroad Commission (RRC) — GIS (gis.rrc.texas.gov) and MFT / public datasets (mft.rrc.texas.gov / RRC public portals)
- New Mexico OCD / EMNRD GIS (gis.emnrd.nm.gov) and BLM right-of-way / realty data
- Oklahoma OCC GIS (gis.occ.ok.gov)
- Louisiana SONRIS (including DOTD-hosted SONRIS map services) and related public exports
- U.S. EIA / HIFLD pipeline data (as used for NM / OK / LA pipeline coverage)
- NETL ArcGIS services (neighbor / pipeline context)
- BSEE and BOEM public well / mapping datasets
- FracFocus public disclosure downloads
- USGS The National Map basemap tiles (basemap.nationalmap.gov) — may be saved on device after Save this view
- Esri ArcGIS Online basemap tiles (server.arcgisonline.com) — Imagery, Streets, Topo; online-only (terms disallow caching)
- OpenStreetMap tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org)
- Apple Maps and Google Maps — opened via system deep links for turn-by-turn (not embedded purchase)

Billing (website only, not inside the iOS binary): Stripe Checkout / Customer Portal on https://wellnav.simba.services in Safari. Stripe never loads in the WKWebView.

No AI / LLM service is used to deliver core functionality.

================================================================================
6. Regulated industry / protected material
================================================================================
Well Navigation compiles publicly available government and open oil-and-gas datasets for workplace lookup. It does not operate wells, does not drill or produce, and does not provide engineering, surveying, or land-title certification. Locations are not a substitute for a One Call / 811 locate, an as-built survey, or operator drawings.

Esri basemap tiles are online-only because Esri’s terms disallow caching them; USGS National Map tiles may be saved on the device after the user taps Save this view. The app does not license or redistribute a proprietary third-party commercial well database.

We are not claiming a special RRC (or other agency) authorization letter beyond ordinary public-data use of published government sources.
