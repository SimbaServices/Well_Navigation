Notes for the reviewer

Well Navigation is workplace software for oil and gas organizations (Guideline 3.1.3(c) enterprise / 3.1.3(b) multiplatform). Employees sign in with their work email to search wells, pipelines, and waste-disposal sites in Texas, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Louisiana.

The iOS app does not sell subscriptions and does not collect payment. There are no In-App Purchases. Organization admins buy seats on the website (https://wellnav.simba.services) in Safari. Stripe Checkout for MP Solutions never loads in the WKWebView. Any URL with ?external_browser=1, plus maps.apple.com / Google Maps, mailto, and tel, open in Safari or the system apps.

The wrapper is not a general-purpose browser (no address bar; host allow-list is wellnav.simba.services only). Field value beyond the website: offline USGS map tiles and pinned wells after the user taps Save this view, a native splash, and an offline banner.

Export compliance: HTTPS only. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false.

Sign in with Apple is not offered because the only login is email and password (no third-party social login).
