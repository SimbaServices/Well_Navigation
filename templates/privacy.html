Privacy policy

Last updated September 17, 2026

This policy describes how Simba Services (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use Well Navigation — the website at wellnav.simba.services, the iOS app, and the Google Play app (together, the “Service”). Seat charges are processed by Stripe for MP Solutions on the website.

Questions: wellnav@simba.services.

1. Who this applies to

The Service is a professional oil and gas workspace. It is not directed at children. Do not create an account if you are under 18.

2. Information we collect

Account

  • Work email address and a hashed password.
  • Organization membership inferred from your email domain (people on the same company domain share an organization; the first account on a domain is the admin).
  • Email one-time codes used to create an account or reset a password.
  • Last login and last activity timestamps.

Workspace data you create

  • Wells you save.
  • Recent searches and a short-lived lookup cache used to speed up the product.

Technical logs

  • Basic server logs: time, request path, and IP address, used to operate and secure the Service.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the iOS or Google Play apps. Website analytics (Hotjar and/or Microsoft Clarity) run on wellnav.simba.services only, not inside those apps.
  • Those apps do not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or device location. Map pins use well, pipeline, and waste-site coordinates from public regulatory sources, not your GPS.
  • We do not collect mobile numbers in the current Service.
  • Payment card numbers are not collected in the iOS or Google Play apps. Well Navigation is a paid workplace product. Billing is handled on the website in Safari or Chrome, not as an in-app purchase.

Well, pipeline, permit, and waste-site records shown in the Service come from public regulatory and published sources. Those records describe industry facilities, not your personal identity, unless you choose to save them to your account.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • Send verification and password-reset codes by email.
  • Remember saved wells and recent lookups.
  • Let coworkers on the same email domain share an organization.
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Understand website usage with Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, and Contentsquare (page views, clicks, and optional session replay on wellnav.simba.services).

The iOS and Google Play apps load the same signed-in workspace over HTTPS. They are not general-purpose browsers and have no address bar.

4. Cookies and session

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in after you enter your email and password. The website may also set Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, or Contentsquare cookies for usage analytics. We do not use advertising cookies. The iOS and Google Play apps do not load those scripts.

5. Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Organization admins on your email domain can see your email and role, manage seats, and change your role or remove you from the organization. They cannot delete your account for you; you delete it yourself.
  • Payments use Stripe on the website (Safari or Chrome) for MP Solutions. Stripe receives the card or wallet details you enter. We receive subscription status, seat quantity, and billing email — not your full card number. The iOS and Google Play apps do not open Stripe Checkout or collect cards.
  • Email delivery uses our mail host (mail.simba.services) to send verification codes.
  • Maps in the browser or app load map tiles from providers such as Esri, OpenStreetMap, or USGS. Those providers may receive the tile request from your device. If you tap Save this view, USGS tiles and the last pipeline/waste-site overlay for that area are stored on your device so the map still works without a signal. Esri tiles are not stored. You can remove that cache with Clear saved maps.
  • Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, and Contentsquare on the website receive page views, clicks, and session recordings so we can improve the product. Those scripts are not loaded in the iOS or Google Play apps.
  • Simba Services UX recordings automatically capture signed-in workspace sessions for @simba.services accounts so we can review the product. Those files are stored on our server. They are not used for customer billing. Passwords are masked.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the Service, our users, or the public.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Retention

We keep account and workspace data while your account is open. Lookup cache entries expire on a short schedule. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for operations and security. When you delete your account, we remove your login, saved wells, and search/cache history associated with that account. Organization records that other people still use are kept. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period until those backups rotate.

7. Account deletion and your choices

You can delete your account on the website or in the iOS or Google Play app: open Account, choose Delete account, enter your password, and type DELETE. That permanently removes your login, saved wells, and search history. Other people in your organization keep their own accounts.

You may also email wellnav@simba.services to ask what personal information we hold, to correct it, or to request deletion if you cannot use the in-app flow.

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” as those terms are defined by statute. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined. We will not discriminate against you for exercising those rights.

8. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not in plaintext. Sessions are sent over HTTPS when the Service is served on wellnav.simba.services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please use a unique password and keep your sign-in details confidential.

9. International users

The Service is operated from the United States and is intended for professional use related to U.S. oil and gas records. If you access it from another country, your information may be processed in the United States.

10. Payments

Well Navigation is a paid professional workspace billed per organization seat. We do not process card numbers inside the iOS or Google Play apps, and those apps do not include in-app purchases. Subscription and seat charges are collected on the website by Stripe for MP Solutions.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
